Kasey Jones

Founder | Director

I’ve seen firsthand how sports can be transformative, and how the right coach can deeply shape an athlete’s journey. From an early age, I developed a strong work ethic and a passion for competition. As a 3-time All-State and All-District athlete in volleyball, swimming, and tennis, and through my experiences in collegiate and professional beach volleyball, I came to understand how athletics build confidence, resilience, and the tools necessary to succeed in life.

My decision to start Athens Elite grew directly from those experiences: the positive ones that fueled my love for the game, and the challenges I faced, particularly at the collegiate level, that revealed what young athletes need most. Now, watching my own daughters discover their love for the game, I’m reminded daily how critical it is to nurture athletes in uplifting, inclusive environments that focus on the whole person.

That’s why I created Athens Elite: a club committed to empowering athletes of all ages and skill levels. Our mission is to build skill, confidence, and a lifelong love for the game. Through technical training, mental resilience, and positive reinforcement, my vision is to help players grow not just as athletes, but as people, developing leaders through volleyball.

Beyond coaching, I serve as a professor in the Game Art & Design program at Columbus College of Art & Design and in the School of Art at Ohio University, where I bring the same passion for growth, creativity, and mentorship into the classroom.

My journey as a volleyball player began on the sun-soaked beaches of Brazil, where my passion for the sport truly blossomed. Over the years, my dedication, relentless hustle, and love for the game led me to win numerous gold and silver medals, cementing my place in the competitive world of beach volleyball.

A fun fact about my volleyball journey: I was so committed to improving my game that I would wake up at 5 am to get in extra practice before heading off to school. That early-morning grind shaped not only my skills on the court but also instilled in me a deep appreciation for hard work and perseverance.

In addition to my playing career, I’ve spent the last two years coaching youth volleyball at Vinton County Middle School. Coaching has allowed me to share my love for the game with the next generation of athletes, helping them hone their skills while fostering their self-esteem and emotional growth. Seeing young players develop both on and off the court is incredibly rewarding, and I take pride in creating a positive environment where they can thrive and build confidence in themselves.

Jeremy Lindauer

Head Coach

12 Regional

Coach Jeremy comes from a background in a variety of sports, but spent much of his sporting career in racing and endurance sports such as swimming, running, and triathlon. Throughout middle and high school Jeremy competed in basketball, football, cross country, track, and swimming, and he went on to pursue swimming in college. At the University of Indianapolis Jeremy was a 5-time NCAA All-American swimmer, an NCAA Academic All-American, and was a conference champion both individually and in relays. Jeremy went on to compete in triathlon after college and won two USA Triathlon National Championships and represented Team USA at the Triathlon World Championships in 2014.

Jeremy has been a coach since 2007 in swimming, triathlon, running, and volleyball, and has coached athletes to National Runner-up, National Championships, and even an individual World Championship in triathlon and multi-sport. Jeremy has coached club swimming for multiple programs in different states, and helped athletes achieve several team record and state-finalist performances along the way. Most recently Jeremy has coached 4th-6th grade rec-league volleyball for 10 seasons, including a 3-peat of league championships!

Jeremy loves helping athletes achieve their goals, whatever those goals may be, and is highly detail-oriented. Beyond all the science, physiology, and nerdy data analytics that he studies in his spare time, Jeremy’s coaching philosophy revolves around a few primary principles which have served him well in his 30+ years of competing and coaching: love the process, master the fundamentals, never stop believing in yourself, and most of all have fun! If an athlete can check those boxes then most of the other elements will fall into place. 

In his free time Jeremy loves spending time with his wife and two daughters, which largely consists of watching, coaching, or just shuttling them to and from all their sporting and theater endeavors.

I played volleyball through elementary and high school. My most memorable moment was making varsity my freshman year.

I have been coaching for 2 years at Alexander and recently became the youth league director Spring 2026.

Investing in youth sports is something I am very passionate about. Foremost I am here to build confidence, integrity, and resilience in our young athletes. Athletes need to know how to forgive not only their teammates mistakes, but their own as well. Next ball mentality!
