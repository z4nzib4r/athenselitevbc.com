About Us

Our Philosophy

At Athens Elite, our philosophy is built on two pillars:

We empower our athletes to lead. We equip our players with the skills, mindset, and resilience they need to succeed not only at the club, high school, and collegiate levels, but also in life beyond the game.

We foster a love for the game. We believe the sport should bring joy. By nurturing gratitude and passion for the game, we create a positive environment where athletes grow, compete, and thrive.

Through teaching the most effective and proven techniques in today’s game, we give our athletes the tools to excel on the court, and the confidence to carry those lessons into everything they do. Our coaching staff brings extensive experience across all levels, including collegiate play, club coaching, and collegiate coaching. This expertise allows us to cultivate athletes who are known for their exceptional character, respect, and competitive spirit, both on and off the court.We believe that the discipline, teamwork, and resilience learned through volleyball are life-long assets, helping our athletes achieve success in every aspect of their lives.
