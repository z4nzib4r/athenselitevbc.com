Athens Elite isn’t just a club—we’re a volleyball program built to mirror collegiate and professional standards.

What sets us apart:

  • Strength and conditioning through our partnership with Ohio Univerisity’s Wellworks
  • On-court training fueled by individual statistics and game film
  • Comprehensive apparel package: backpack, two jerseys, two practice t-shirts, and warm-ups
  • New this year: mental skills coaching—because the mental game matters as much as physical skill

We’re committed to developing the whole athlete—mind, body, and game. If you’re ready to elevate your performance from every angle, we want you at tryouts!
