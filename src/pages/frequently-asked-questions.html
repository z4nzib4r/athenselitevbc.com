Q: What is club volleyball?

Club volleyball is the next step in an athlete’s journey to develop their skills and compete at a higher level. Club volleyball is competitive and requires travel to tournaments, with most tournaments taking place within the region.

Below is an overview of what is included in your club fees.

On-Court Training

  • 2 times per week at Ping Recreation Center
  • Saturday: 10:00 AM–12:00 PM
  • Sunday: 12:30–2:30 PM

Strength & Conditioning

  • 2 times per week: Saturdays before practice (9:30–10:00 AM) and Sundays after practice (2:30–3:00 PM)

Mental Training Workshops

  • Provided by North Texas Sports Counseling

Film & Stat Review

  • Through our partnership with HUDL

Semester Membership

  • Semester membership to Ping Recreation Center

Apparel Package

  • Backpack, 2 jerseys and a warm-up

Q: What is Winter In-House League?

Our Winter In-House League is a skill-development-focused program for athletes in grades 3–7 who want to learn and strengthen their volleyball techniques in a fun, low-stakes environment. Led by Athens Elite Owner and Director Kasey Jones, the program focuses on developing technical mastery of volleyball’s core mechanics while helping athletes build confidence, stay active, connect with their community, and develop a love for the game.

Program Cost: $800
Includes coaching, court space, equipment, and an Athens Elite jersey.

The league runs every Wednesday from January through April 10, with games every other Saturday beginning the first weekend in February.

In summary:

  • Club Volleyball = competitive play, travel tournaments, higher commitment
  • In-House League = skill-building, local gameplay, no travel

Q: Where are practices held?

All practices are held at Ping Recreation Center, 82 South Green Drive, Athens, Ohio 45701.

Q: Where are tournaments?

It depends on the team’s level:

  • Regional Level stays within the region, about 1–2 hours from Athens, and plays mostly 1-day tournaments.
  • American Level travels a bit further, about 3–6 hours from Athens, and plays both 1-day and 2-day tournaments.
  • National Level travels the furthest, mostly out of state, and plays mostly 2-day tournaments.

Q: How much does travel volleyball cost?

Club fees depend on the team’s level:

  • Regional Level: $1,200
  • American Level: $1,800
  • National Level: $2,400
