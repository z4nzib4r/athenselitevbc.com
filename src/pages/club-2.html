Our Philosophy

At Athens Elite, our philosophy is centered on empowering athletes with the skills and mindset they need to succeed not just at the club, high school, and collegiate levels, but also in life. By teaching the most effective and proven techniques in today’s game, we provide our players with the tools they need to thrive on the court and beyond.

Our coaching staff brings extensive experience across all levels, including collegiate play, club coaching, and collegiate coaching. This expertise allows us to cultivate athletes who are known for their exceptional character, respect, and competitive spirit, both on and off the court.

We believe that the discipline, teamwork, and resilience learned through volleyball are life-long assets, helping our athletes achieve success in every aspect of their lives.

Partnerships

Athens Elite has officially partnered with WellWorks, Ohio University’s premier wellness program. Our collaboration will allow their expert staff to design our strength and conditioning program for our 2024-2025 club season.
