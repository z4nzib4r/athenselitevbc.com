Summer Sand Series | 2v2 Beach Volleyball Training

The Athens Elite Summer Sand Series is a 2v2 beach volleyball training program designed for athletes who are serious about improving their game in a high-repetition, competitive environment.

Beach volleyball is one of the most effective ways to develop ball control, court awareness, and volleyball IQ. With only two athletes on the court, players are required to perform every skill—pass, set, hit, and defend—leading to faster development and a deeper understanding of the game. Research also supports that beach training can improve strength, endurance, and overall performance, with benefits that transfer directly to the indoor game.

Program Details:

  • 18 training sessions (1.5 hours each)
  • Tuesdays & Thursdays | 9:00–10:30 AM
  • June–July 2026
  • South Green Courts (Athens, OH)

Training Focus:
Ball control, defensive range, court awareness, offensive strategy, and competitive 2v2 gameplay

Cost: $450

Athletes may sign up with a partner of their choice or will be paired with someone of a similar skill level. Participation is limited to ensure a focused, high-quality training environment.

This program is built for consistent development over time, helping athletes become more confident, skilled, and well-rounded players. Spots are limited.
