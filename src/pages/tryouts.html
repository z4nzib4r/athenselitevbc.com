Tryout Dates & Details

Athens Elite is a member of USAV, OVR, AAU, and JVA. In addition to offering a wide variety of tournament options, athletes also receive an extensive player package which includes:

1. On-court training 2x per week at Ping Recreation Center

2. Apparel package that includes a backpack, 2 jersey’s, warm-up, and 2 practice t-shirts.

3. Mental Performance Coaching Workshops with North Texas Sports Counseling

Register for our tryouts by following the steps listed below: 

1. USAV Tryout Membership – $10  (click here)

2. USAV Medical Waiver (download form click here & bring to tryouts)

3. Complete google registration 10U – 12U form

13U Form

4. Submit tryout fee to Athens Elite – $20
