Making Volleyball Accessible

Athens Elite Volleyball offers a limited number of scholarships each season, awarded at the discretion of the Club Director. Scholarships are based on a combination of need and circumstance, as we strive to make participation possible for as many athletes as we can.

If you would like to be considered for a scholarship, please contact us to share a bit more about your situation. While funds are limited, we do our best each year to support families where we can.
