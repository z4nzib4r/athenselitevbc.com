Our Story

Athens Elite was born out of the necessity for top-tier coaching and training programs in southeast Ohio. The foundation of our program is designed to develop leaders through the sport of volleyball. Our core mission extends beyond athletic performance. We are committed to cultivating the complete athlete, nurturing their physical, mental, and emotional well-being for success on and off the court. We elicit passion, confidence, and respect by demonstrating the same in a positive and supportive environment where young athletes thrive.

Our Mission

Athens Elite’s core vision is to develop leaders through the sport of volleyball. We will achieve our vision by:

  1. Providing a competitive and supportive environment for youth to compete at the highest level their abilities and commitment allow
  2. We nurture the whole athlete -physically, mentally, and emotionally- for their enduring success on and off the court
  3. We provide a positive and supportive environment where athletes cultivate their passion and confidence, all while demonstrating a high level of respect and kindness towards their teammates and the game.

How We Achieve Our Mission

We achieve this through targeted goals that encompass the complete athletic experience:

  • Strength & Conditioning: We offer specialized training to build the necessary power, agility, and endurance to excel on the court.
  • Individualized & Group Skills Training: Our coaches focus on developing a strong foundation rooted in proper technique and form, tailored to each athlete’s needs.
  • Game-Like Drills (“Grills”): Practices simulate game-like situations, preparing athletes to think and react under game pressure.
  • Mental Toughness Strategies: We equip athletes with tools to cope with high-pressure situations, both during training and in competition.
  • Nutrition & Mental Wellness: During the season, we emphasize the importance of proper nutrition and mental health, helping athletes maintain peak performance and balance.

By incorporating these elements, Athens Elite ensures that each athlete develops the physical, technical, and mental resilience required to succeed both in volleyball and in life.
