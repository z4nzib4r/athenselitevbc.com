Winter In-House Coed League

Click here to register

If you are looking for a place to get your child started in club without the travel and time commitment of a travel team, then our in-house, club prep program is the perfect option for you. The goal of our in-house, club prep league is to bridge the gap between full club and rec volleyball. This program will help give athletes the skill, knowledge and confidence to help them achieve their volleyball goals, such as making future teams, whether that be school or club. Our program focuses on building strong foundational skills by teaching proper technique. We emphasize both individual skill development all while training athletes within a competitive, team-oriented environment. This program is designed to closely mirror the club experience and is open to all athletes looking to improve their volleyball skills.

The league runs Wednesday, January 6th, 2027 – Saturday, April 10th, 2027. All teams will practice every Wednesday starting Wednesday, January 6th, 2027. Practice times are 4:30pm-5:45pm or 5:45pm-7:00pm depending on which team your child is on. Each team will play every other Saturday morning starting Saturday, February 6th, 2027. Saturday competition days will be divided into two waves: 9:30am -10:45 am, 3rd-4th grades, and 10:45 am-12:00 pm, 5th – 6th grades.

Divisions: 3rd-4th, 5th – 6th Grades
