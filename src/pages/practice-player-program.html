What the Program Includes

  • Training alongside an assigned Athens Elite club team
  • Additional skill development and scrimmage opportunities
  • Strength and conditioning and mental training
  • Club gear and participation in club-wide events
  • No tournament travel or competition
  • Placement based on athlete development, team needs, and coach recommendations
