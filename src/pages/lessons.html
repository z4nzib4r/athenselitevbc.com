Private & Small Group Lessons

Athens Elite offers private and small group volleyball lessons designed to meet the needs of each athlete. Whether you’re looking for one-on-one coaching or want to train with friends, we’ve got you covered.

Our rates are as follows:

$100 for 1 athlete (1 hour)

$75 per athlete for 2 athletes (1 hour)

$65 per athlete for 3 athletes (1.5-2 hours)

$55 per athlete for 4 athletes (2 hours)

$50 per athlete for 5-6 athletes (2 hours)

Cancellation Policy

We require at least 48 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule a lesson. Cancellations made with less than 48 hours’ notice, and missed lessons, will be charged the full lesson fee.
