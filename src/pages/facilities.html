Ping Recreation Center

Athens Elite is proud to practice at the Ping Recreation Center, a world-class facility located at Ohio University. Known for its top-tier amenities and spacious courts, Ping provides the perfect environment for our athletes to refine their volleyball skills. This modern and well-maintained facility supports our club’s commitment to excellence, offering an outstanding space for training and teamwork.

Ping Recreation Center

WellWorks Training Center

Athens Elite is pleased to announce an official partnership with WellWorks, Ohio University’s wellness program. This collaboration will allow WellWorks’ experienced staff to develop a specialized strength and conditioning program for our 2024-2025 club season. By integrating their expertise with our club’s training goals, we are set to enhance our athletes’ development and performance.
