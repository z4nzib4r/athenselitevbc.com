I am excited to announce that Athens Elite Volleyball Club is holding open summer tryouts for the 2024-2025 club season, 15 Girls age division! We are looking for committed players who are focused on taking their skills and gameplay to the next level.

Tryout Details:

Date: Saturday, July 20th

Time: 1:00 – 3:00 PM

Location: Ping Recreation Center, 82 South Green Drive, Athens, Ohio

Tryout fee: $20

To register for tryouts, please click the link below to access our registration form. As members of USAV and AAU, we aim to offer a wide variety of tournament options in addition to our competitive player packages.

Our player packages include:

1. 6 months membership to Ping Recreation Center

2. Strength and Conditioning 2x per week

3. On-court training 2x per week

4. Apparel package that includes a ball, backpack, 2 jersey’s, warm-up, and 2 practice t-shirts.

We are dedicated to establishing Athens Elite VBC as a premier volleyball club in the heart of Athens, Ohio. Please join us for our inaugural season today!
